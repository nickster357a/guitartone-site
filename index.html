The Impact of Wood on a Guitars Tone

How Wood Effects Your Sound.

Mahogany

Mahogany is known for its warm, dark tones. It provides a punchy sound with great sustain, making it a staple for blues and rock guitarists.

Maple

Maple is a dense wood that produces a bright, snappy high-end with clear note definition, making it perfect for lead playing.
